a web application designed to support Vietnamese history research by connecting historical statements with relevant passages from digitized historical documents.

Instead of simply presenting search results, the application helps users explore the original historical sources, navigate to relevant PDF pages, and examine highlighted evidence in context.

### How It Works

The application follows a workflow that connects user-submitted historical content with digitized historical sources.

- Submit Historical Text: Users enter a historical passage or select a predefined example.

- Analyze and Extract Claims: The backend processes the submitted content and identifies individual historical claims.

- Retrieve Evidence: The backend searches its historical document collection for relevant passages.

- Explore Evidence: The frontend displays the extracted claims and their corresponding source passages.

- Verify Against Original Documents: Users can open the relevant PDF excerpt, navigate to the target page, and inspect highlighted text.

The application is designed to assist historical research. Retrieved evidence should be interpreted in its original context, and the absence of a matching passage does not necessarily mean that a historical claim is false.
